Meet the global team

RLadies+ Global is run by a volunteer group with designated responsibilities within the organisation. The leadership team provides overall direction — keeping the organisation well-structured, honouring commitments to sponsors and members, and guiding where RLadies+ grows next.

If you would like to join the global team, the RLadies+ Guide covers what the work involves. Take a look at our current vacancies and fill out the interest form for any roles that appeal to you.
